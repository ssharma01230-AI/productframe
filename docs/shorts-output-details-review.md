# Shorts Output Details — visual review

Draft prompt text for review only. No application integration is included.

Scope: all 9 currently frontend-wired Ecommerce templates for the `shorts` family in `apps/web/app/studio/output-recipes.ts`. These are the subtype-specific image packs; the shared Lifestyle and Campaign recipes are outside this document. Slot ordering, IDs and asset references below match that mapping. Every referenced image was visually inspected.

The uploaded product remains authoritative for colour, material, length, fit and construction. Benchmark colours and features are not mandatory: do not add pockets, cords, pleats, closures or cuffs that the product does not have, or remove ones it does. Supporting tops and footwear are secondary styling. Where a pose uses pockets, use relaxed hands beside the garment if the product has none. Do not infer an unseen rear design from a front image.

The requested background is light warm beige, approximately #C8C1B6, replacing the benchmark's existing white/grey surface where necessary. This is draft output direction, not a claim that the image assets have been changed.

Review note: slots 06 and 08 show neck-to-feet compositions, not the model's head. Slot 09 is product-only, not a worn waist detail. There is no tenth template in this pack.
