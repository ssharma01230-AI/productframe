# Shirts Output Details — visual review

Draft prompt text for review only. No application integration is included.

Scope: every currently frontend-wired template. Review in progress; this document currently covers the 14 Shirts ecommerce templates. Other families, generic fallback recipes, Lifestyle and Campaign remain to be reviewed.

The frontend mapping in `apps/web/app/studio/output-recipes.ts` determines slot numbers. Images are inspected visually; filenames and current names are identifiers, not evidence of composition. Shirts slots 01–14 map to asset filenames 02–15 respectively.

Use the uploaded product's identity, material, colour and construction. The white shirt pictured in these examples is a composition benchmark, not a requirement to turn other shirts white or add its pockets/buttons. Backgrounds below use the requested light warm beige approximately #C8C1B6 wherever exposed studio background is visible. Macro images remain filled by the actual product material.

- Framing: Portrait crop from the lower face to the upper thighs, including both hands.
- Crop: Exclude the eyes and top of the head; retain the collar, shoulders, shirt front and both cuffs. The hem is naturally concealed by the tuck.
- Product position: Torso centred, arms relaxed alongside the body.
- Product scale: Shirt fills most of the image width; trousers provide a small amount of styling context below the waist.
- Silhouette: Preserve the product's fit and shoulder/sleeve proportions with natural folds at the tuck.
- Visible construction: Show the collar, front closure, sleeves, cuffs and supported front details clearly.
- Garment volume: Natural worn chest and sleeve volume, with soft folds at the elbows and waist.
- Background: Light warm beige studio backdrop approximately #C8C1B6.
- Lighting: Soft diffuse studio light with gentle form shadows and clear fabric detail.
- Colour treatment: Preserve the uploaded shirt's true colour and finish; prevent beige light spill from tinting it.
- Composition: One standing model, no additional subjects; tailored trousers and belt remain secondary.
- Output: One continuous high-fidelity ecommerce photograph, without insets or additional views.

## Review notes for these 14 images

- The benchmark for slot 08 includes a chest pocket; several other shirts examples do not. Product identity must always come from the upload.
- Slots 01 and 04 visibly contain parts of a face. Slot 14 includes the lower back of the head. The descriptions above record that observed framing rather than claiming all heads/faces are entirely excluded.
- Slots 01, 05, 11 and 14 conceal the hem with a tuck; requiring the complete hem to remain visible would contradict those compositions.
- Slot 06 includes a tie, trousers and chair context; slot 05 includes a stool. A universal ban on all secondary clothing/props would conflict with these images.
- Slot 07 fills the frame with fabric and cannot simultaneously expose a beige studio backdrop.
- The supplied sample's fixed white colour, straight hem, invisible-mannequin mode and no-human/no-crop rules cannot be copied unchanged into all templates. These output sections describe composition while leaving product identity to the uploaded product.
