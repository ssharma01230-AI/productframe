The Casual bottoms family currently uses the joggers benchmark as its nine
benchmark-mapped Ecommerce templates from
`apps/web/public/output-examples/bottoms/joggers/`. Template IDs are stable and
version 2.

The benchmark composition shows soft cream joggers with an elastic drawstring
waistband, angled pockets, tapered legs, ribbed ankle cuffs, plain white
T-shirts for model views and neutral white footwear. Product references remain
authoritative for identity; these assets control composition and presentation.
