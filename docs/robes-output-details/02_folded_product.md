## Benchmark observations

The benchmark shows a robe folded into a compact, symmetrical rectangle on a warm beige surface. The emerald satin material, shawl collar and belt arrangement are non-authoritative.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** One uploaded robe product, neatly folded and presented without a model, mannequin or visible support.
- **Presentation:** Product-only folded ecommerce composition. Show a single coherent garment, not packaging, a stack or multiple colourways.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Near-overhead to slightly elevated front view, with minimal perspective and square, orderly geometry.
- **Framing and crop:** Entire folded robe visible with comfortable warm-neutral margin on every side. Do not crop the fold, collar, belt or outer edges.
- **Arrangement:** Fold the robe into a compact vertical rectangle while keeping the evidenced collar or neckline and representative front material visible. Tuck sleeves and lower body cleanly beneath. Include a belt across the fold only if the uploaded product contains one; otherwise use a clean belt-free fold.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the folded robe and let it occupy roughly two-thirds to three-quarters of the frame height. Maintain plausible folded thickness and proportions.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Preserve credible fabric volume and soft fold compression. Avoid rigid box-like edges, implausible symmetry or excessive puffiness.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve only evidenced collar or neckline, front edge, trim, belt, loops, pockets, labels and seams that would remain visible under this fold. Do not invent construction merely to resemble the benchmark.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve actual colour, pattern, texture, weave or knit, opacity, lustre and fold behaviour. Do not convert matte fabric to satin or transfer emerald colour.
- **Background:** Flat warm light beige studio surface, approximately `#C8C1B6`, clean and untextured.
- **Lighting:** Soft elevated lighting with gentle edge shadows and controlled material highlights. Avoid harsh specular clipping and dramatic directional shadow.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Folded-away construction is not a license to invent hidden details. If the product evidence cannot support a convincing collar-forward fold, retain the actual neckline and use the closest physically plausible compact fold.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no person, mannequin, hanger, packaging, collage, inset, text or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm the entire fold is visible, the arrangement is physically plausible, and a belt, collar treatment or satin sheen appears only when supported by the uploaded robe.
