## Source evidence and uncertainty

- Recommended evidence tags: `front_view`.
- Visual coverage needed for faithful reproduction: Head-on front reference resolving toe shape and fastening.
- Evidence tags alone do not establish anatomical side, sufficient resolution or complete coverage; inspect the actual image.
- Never transfer logos, seams or decoration onto an unseen side, or treat unknown surfaces as blank. Do not invent tread, rear branding or insole labels from this benchmark.
- Outer and inner mean lateral and medial anatomy, not simply left/right image direction. Do not mirror logos or lettering. For pairs, preserve left/right asymmetry rather than duplicating one shoe.
- If Continue anyway is used without recommended evidence, retain the missing-reference disclaimer. Inferred details remain unverified. This document does not change readiness gates.
