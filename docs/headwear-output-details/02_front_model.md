# Front Model

- **Global category:** `accessories`
- **Eligible family:** `headwear` only
- **Channel:** `ecommerce`
- **Presentation mode:** `model`; adult model
- **Benchmark:** [View exact image](references/02.png)

## Benchmark observations

The benchmark is a centred head-and-shoulders portrait of an adult model facing camera. The entire cap is visible, the peak sits above the eyes, and the crop extends through the upper chest. The grey crew-neck top, model identity, navy cap, curved peak and embroidered artwork are non-authoritative.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded headwear worn correctly by one adult model in a neutral front ecommerce portrait.
- **Presentation:** Centred head-and-shoulders model view. Keep clothing plain and visually subordinate; no jewellery, glasses or accessories that obscure the product unless required for correct product use.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Level, straight-on portrait camera at eye height with minimal lens distortion.
- **Framing and crop:** Include the complete headwear with clear margin above and on both sides, plus the model from head through shoulders or upper chest. Do not crop the brim, crown, side edges or product appendages.
- **Pose:** Model faces directly forward with head level, shoulders relaxed and a neutral expression. The product sits in its intended orientation and position.
- **Product position and scale:** Headwear is the principal product and must be large enough to inspect while retaining enough head and shoulders to communicate fit.
- **Fit and silhouette:** Preserve actual crown height and volume, brim or edge angle, depth on the head, coverage of forehead and ears, and relationship to the wearer's head. Avoid floating, clipping, compression or oversized fit.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve evidenced front panels or shaping, seams, top details, ventilation, brim stitching, logos, embroidery, prints, patches and trim. Do not invent baseball-cap features for another headwear type.
- **Artwork fidelity:** Preserve exact evidenced artwork content, placement, scale, orientation, spelling, colours and production method. Do not reproduce benchmark artwork.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve actual colour, pattern, weave or knit, texture, distressing, sheen and edge finish under realistic worn tension.
- **Background:** Warm light beige seamless studio background, approximately `#C8C1B6`, with no contextual props.
- **Lighting:** Soft diffuse portrait-catalogue lighting that keeps the product evenly readable while rendering skin naturally. Avoid deep shadow under a brim that hides product fit or dominates the face.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not infer side or rear artwork, hidden closure, interior structure or labels from the front. Model identity and clothing are compositional and must not be copied as product attributes.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no collage, inset, duplicate view, text overlay or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm the complete product remains in frame, fit is physically credible, the model is directly front-facing, and all construction and artwork follow the upload.
