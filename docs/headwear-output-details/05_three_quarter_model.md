# Three-Quarter Model

- **Global category:** `accessories`
- **Eligible family:** `headwear` only
- **Channel:** `ecommerce`
- **Presentation mode:** `model`; adult model
- **Benchmark:** [View exact image](references/05.png)

## Benchmark observations

The benchmark is a close head-and-shoulders portrait at a front three-quarter angle, with the model looking across the frame. The composition reveals the cap front, peak curvature, one side of the crown and the transition toward the rear. The model identity, grey top, navy washed fabric and embroidered artwork are non-authoritative.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded headwear worn correctly by one adult model in a restrained front three-quarter ecommerce portrait.
- **Presentation:** Single model with plain, subordinate clothing and no competing accessories. Choose the three-quarter side best supported by the uploaded product evidence.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Level camera at head height, approximately 30–45 degrees off the front. Avoid drifting into a near-front or strict side view.
- **Framing and crop:** Include the complete product with clear margin around the crown, brim, peak or appendages, plus the model through shoulders or upper chest.
- **Pose:** Head turned naturally to the selected three-quarter angle, posture upright, shoulders relaxed and expression neutral. No hands touch or obscure the product.
- **Product position and scale:** Make the headwear the primary focus while retaining enough facial and head context to communicate fit.
- **Fit and silhouette:** Preserve actual crown structure and height, brim or edge projection and curvature, side depth, coverage and alignment on the head. Avoid warping logos around the crown or changing brim symmetry.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve evidenced front and visible-side panels or shaping, seams, ventilation, top details, brim stitching, logos, embroidery, patches, trim and any closure portion naturally visible. Do not combine incompatible details from opposite sides.
- **Artwork fidelity:** Maintain correct placement, perspective, spelling, scale, colours, borders and production method. Do not mirror artwork or substitute the benchmark design.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve actual colour, pattern, texture, weave or knit, nap, wash, distressing, sheen and realistic worn tension.
- **Background:** Warm light beige seamless studio background, approximately `#C8C1B6`, without contextual props.
- **Lighting:** Soft diffuse portrait-catalogue lighting that describes crown volume, peak or edge geometry and surface texture without hard facial or product shadows.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not invent the partially seen rear, hidden side artwork, closure or interior. If asymmetry is not documented for the chosen side, request or rely on matching-side evidence.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no collage, inset, duplicate view, text overlay or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm the angle is clearly three-quarter, the complete product is visible, fit and perspective are credible, and no side-specific artwork or construction has been mirrored or invented.
