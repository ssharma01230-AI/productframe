## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded headwear worn correctly by one adult model in a strict side-profile ecommerce portrait.
- **Presentation:** Single model with plain, subordinate clothing. Use the product side for which uploaded evidence exists; do not mirror one side when the product is asymmetric.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Level 90-degree side camera at head height. The face and head point directly toward the selected side without three-quarter rotation.
- **Framing and crop:** Include the complete headwear, full front-to-rear projection and the model from head through shoulder or upper torso. Retain margin beyond any brim, peak, ties, flap or rear hardware.
- **Pose:** Upright side profile with neck long, shoulders relaxed and gaze aligned forward relative to the model. No hand contact with the product.
- **Product position and scale:** Keep the headwear prominent while showing enough head and ear relationship to establish fit and coverage.
- **Fit and silhouette:** Preserve actual crown contour, profile height, brim or peak length and curve, rear depth, ear clearance and attachment angle. Avoid perspective shortening or an implausibly floating fit.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve evidenced side panels or shaping, seams, ventilation, side artwork, brim edge and stitching, rear opening, closure and hardware visible from this side. Do not expose or invent details unsupported by a matching side or rear reference.
- **Artwork fidelity:** Maintain the correct side, placement, reading direction, scale, spelling, colours and production method. Never mirror logos or move front artwork onto the side.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve actual material, colour, pattern, texture, nap, wash, distressing, sheen and wear response.
- **Background:** Warm light beige seamless studio background, approximately `#C8C1B6`, without props.
- **Lighting:** Broad soft side-balanced lighting that separates the silhouette and keeps dark surfaces, seams and hardware readable.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Side-specific artwork and closure visibility require corresponding evidence. If only the opposite side is supplied, do not assume symmetry or mirror text and graphics.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no collage, inset, duplicate view, text overlay or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm the view is a true 90-degree profile, the selected side is evidence-backed, no logo is mirrored, and the front-to-rear silhouette matches the uploaded product.
