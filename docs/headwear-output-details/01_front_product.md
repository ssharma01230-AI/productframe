## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** One uploaded headwear product shown alone in a clean front ecommerce view.
- **Presentation:** Product-only presentation with no model, mannequin, head form, stand, packaging or styling accessory visible.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Level, straight-on camera centred on the product's front. Use a slightly elevated viewpoint only where needed to describe the crown without changing the front-view character.
- **Framing and crop:** Keep the complete product visible with comfortable warm-neutral margin on every side. Include the full crown, brim, peak or edge, plus any appendages that belong to the product.
- **Arrangement:** Orient the true front directly toward camera and support the product invisibly so its natural worn geometry remains credible. Do not flatten a structured product or artificially inflate a soft one.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the headwear in the lower-middle of the portrait frame, at a scale large enough to inspect front construction and artwork.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Preserve actual crown height, width, structure, brim or peak projection, curvature, edge profile and symmetry or intended asymmetry.
- **Visible construction:** Reproduce only evidenced panels, seams, darts, top button, ventilation holes, brim stitching, bindings, logos, embroidery, prints, patches, hardware and trim. Do not turn other headwear into a baseball cap or invent benchmark-specific construction.
- **Artwork fidelity:** Preserve exact placement, scale, spelling, letterforms, colours, borders and stitch or print character of evidenced branding. Never substitute the benchmark's `C` or `CLASSIC ATHLETIC CLUB` artwork.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve actual colour, pattern, weave or knit, nap, wash, distressing, sheen and edge finish. Do not transfer the benchmark's faded navy cotton texture.
- **Background:** Warm light beige seamless studio background and surface, approximately `#C8C1B6`, free of props and texture.
- **Lighting:** Soft diffuse catalogue lighting with gentle grounding shadow and enough side modelling to reveal shape, seams and material without harsh glare.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** The front view cannot establish hidden rear closures, interior labels or side and rear artwork. Leave those unknown rather than inventing them.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no model, visible support, collage, inset, duplicate view, text overlay or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm the complete silhouette is visible, the view is truly front-facing, and type, structure, brim, construction and artwork come from the uploaded product rather than the benchmark.
