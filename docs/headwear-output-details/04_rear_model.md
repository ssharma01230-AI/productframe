# Rear Model

- **Global category:** `accessories`
- **Eligible family:** `headwear` only
- **Channel:** `ecommerce`
- **Presentation mode:** `model`; adult model viewed from the rear
- **Benchmark:** [View exact image](references/04.png)

## Benchmark observations

The benchmark is a centred rear head-and-shoulders portrait. It clearly shows the back crown, centre seams, ventilation, arched opening and fabric adjustment strap with metal buckle. The model identity, grey top, navy washed fabric, opening and buckle construction are non-authoritative.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded headwear worn correctly by one adult model and shown directly from the rear.
- **Presentation:** Centred rear fit view with simple subordinate clothing. Arrange hair only as needed to keep the product's rear edge and closure readable; do not alter the product to accommodate hair.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Level, straight-on rear camera at head height, centred on the back of the headwear.
- **Framing and crop:** Include the entire product with margin above and at both sides, plus the model from head through shoulders or upper chest. Preserve complete rear appendages, ties, flap, strap or hardware.
- **Pose:** Model faces directly away with head upright and shoulders level. No hand contact or rotation toward profile.
- **Product position and scale:** Headwear remains dominant and large enough to inspect rear construction, while the head and neck communicate fit.
- **Fit and silhouette:** Preserve actual rear crown shape, depth, lower edge, head coverage, opening shape and relationship to ears and hair.
- **Visible construction:** Reproduce only evidenced rear panels, seams, ventilation, closures, adjustment straps, elastic, toggles, buckles, openings, labels, logos and edge finishing. Do not invent the benchmark's arched opening or brass-toned buckle.
- **Artwork and hardware fidelity:** Preserve exact rear branding and hardware shape, material, finish, colour, placement and fastening path. Do not extrapolate front artwork to the rear.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve actual colour, pattern, weave or knit, nap, wash, distressing, sheen and tension around the closure.
- **Background:** Warm light beige seamless studio background, approximately `#C8C1B6`, with no props.
- **Lighting:** Soft diffuse catalogue lighting with controlled edge definition and enough fill to reveal dark rear seams and closure hardware.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** A rear product reference is required for definitive rear construction. If absent, withhold or flag this view rather than fabricate closure type, opening, seams, branding or hardware.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no visible face, collage, inset, front-view details, text overlay or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm the model faces directly away, all rear extremities remain visible, hair does not hide construction, and closure, seams, hardware and branding are grounded in rear evidence.
