# Full Front Model

- **Global category:** `tailoring`
- **Eligible family:** `suit-jackets` only
- **Channel:** `ecommerce`
- **Presentation mode:** `model`; adult model with face and facial features excluded
- **Benchmark:** [View image](references/03.png)

## Benchmark observations

The benchmark defines a full front model composition and catalogue scale only. Its jacket identity, colour, fabric, tailoring, shirt, trousers, shoes and model identity must not be copied.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded suit jacket worn by one adult model.
- Presentation: One adult model following the benchmark pose and crop; face and facial features excluded.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Level, straight-on front view with ordinary catalogue perspective; avoid low angle and wide-angle distortion.
- **Framing and crop:** Full model presentation from the base of the neck through the feet where shown by the benchmark, keeping the complete jacket collar, sleeves, cuffs and hem inside frame. Exclude face and facial features.
- **Pose:** Neutral upright standing pose, shoulders level, arms naturally lowered and hands away from pockets unless the benchmark clearly requires otherwise. No exaggerated movement.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the jacket and preserve its real length, width, sleeve proportions and fit. Do not stretch the garment to match another model's body.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Preserve the actual shoulder construction, chest structure, waist shape, hem, sleeve shape and drape. Do not turn a standalone jacket into a full suit, tuxedo, coat or waistcoat.
- **Visible construction:** Show and preserve the uploaded lapels, gorge, front closure, button count, buttonholes, chest and lower pockets, cuffs, vents and seams where evidenced.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve the actual colour, pattern, weave, texture, sheen, wrinkles, fading and tonal variation. Do not transfer benchmark fabric or neutralise visible product variation.
- **Secondary styling:** Neutral shirt, trousers and understated footwear may be present but remain subordinate. Do not invent branded accessories or a coordinated suit when only a jacket is evidenced.
- **Background:** Warm neutral seamless studio setting with a controlled floor or shadow only where required by the composition; no location scenery or distracting props.
- **Lighting:** Soft even catalogue lighting with readable tailoring edges and gentle contact shadows. No hard flash, gels or artificial gloss.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not infer unseen back construction, lining, labels, internal canvas, vents or hardware. Preserve only details supported by the uploaded product images.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph; no collage, inset, duplicate view, text or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm full jacket visibility, face exclusion, credible model anatomy, product-dominant styling and accurate lapel, closure, pocket, cuff and hem construction.
