# Back Model

- **Global category:** `tailoring`
- **Eligible family:** `suit-jackets` only
- **Channel:** `ecommerce`
- **Presentation mode:** `model`; adult model with face and facial features excluded
- **Benchmark:** [View image](references/04.png)

## Benchmark observations

The benchmark provides a rear model angle and framing reference. Its jacket construction, colour, fabric, vents, model and styling are not evidence for the uploaded product.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded suit jacket worn by one adult model.
- Presentation: One adult model following the benchmark pose and crop; face and facial features excluded.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Straight-on rear view at torso height, or the precise restrained rear angle shown by the benchmark. Keep the camera level and avoid wide-angle distortion.
- **Framing and crop:** Show the complete rear jacket from collar through hem, including shoulders, sleeves, cuffs, back panels and vents where visible. Exclude face, facial features and top of head.
- **Pose:** Upright natural stance with shoulders level, arms relaxed and hands outside pockets. No exaggerated twist or body emphasis.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the rear silhouette and preserve the jacket's actual proportions, length, shoulder width and sleeve length.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Preserve the uploaded back shape, shoulder roll, waist suppression, hem and sleeve drape. Do not invent a vent, yoke, pleat or panel.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve only evidenced rear collar, seams, vents, back panels, sleeve seams, cuffs, labels and lining. Do not copy unseen benchmark details.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve the uploaded rear surface's real colour, pattern, weave, nap, sheen, wrinkles and wear. Do not mirror front decoration onto the back.
- **Secondary styling:** Neutral trousers and footwear may be visible only as restrained support. No distracting accessories or extra garments.
- **Background:** Warm neutral seamless studio background with no location scenery or unrelated props.
- **Lighting:** Soft directional catalogue lighting that keeps rear seams, vents and fabric relief readable without dramatic contrast or sharpening.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Rear construction requires rear product evidence. Details not visible in the uploaded rear reference remain unknown rather than plain or copied from the benchmark.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph; no collage, inset, duplicate view, text or watermark.

## Review checks

Verify rear orientation, complete jacket outline, face exclusion, accurate vent and seam placement, and no front construction copied onto unsupported rear surfaces.
