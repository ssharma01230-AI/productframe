# Front Product

- **Global category:** `tailoring`
- **Eligible family:** `suit-jackets` only
- **Channel:** `ecommerce`
- **Presentation mode:** `invisible_mannequin`; no person or visible support
- **Benchmark:** [View image](references/08.png)

## Benchmark observations

The benchmark defines a clean front product presentation. Its jacket colour, material, lapels, buttons, pockets and proportions are reference-specific and must not override the uploaded product.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded suit jacket as a clean product-only front presentation.
- Presentation: Product shaped by a completely invisible mannequin; no body, torso, neck, head or support visible.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Direct straight-on front camera at garment mid-height; no perspective tilt or wide-angle distortion.
- **Framing and crop:** Complete jacket from collar through hem with both sleeves and cuffs visible, balanced modest margins and no identity-critical construction cropped away.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the jacket and match the benchmark's occupancy while preserving the real garment's length, width and proportions.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Show natural tailored shoulder, chest and sleeve volume with a hollow neck opening and no visible support. Preserve the actual fit and hem shape.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve the uploaded lapels, gorge, closure, buttons, buttonholes, chest and lower pockets, cuffs, vents, seams and lining edges where evidenced.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve the product's actual colour, pattern, weave, texture, sheen, wrinkles and tonal variation. Do not transfer benchmark identity or generic tailoring texture.
- **Background:** Warm neutral seamless studio background with a subtle controlled contact shadow and no location scenery or props.
- **Lighting:** Soft even catalogue lighting with gentle directional shadows, readable fabric relief and no dramatic colour treatment or sharpening.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not invent hidden lining, internal canvas, rear details, vents, labels or hardware. Unseen details remain unknown.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no person, mannequin, visible support, text, watermark, collage, inset or duplicate view.

## Review checks

Confirm complete front coverage, accurate product identity, invisible support, credible proportions and clean product-only presentation.
