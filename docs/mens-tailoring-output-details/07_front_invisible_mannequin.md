## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded suit jacket presented on a fully invisible mannequin.
- Presentation: Product shaped by a completely invisible mannequin; no body, torso, neck, head or support visible.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Straight-on front view at garment mid-height with ordinary catalogue perspective.
- **Framing and crop:** Show the complete jacket from collar through hem, including both sleeves, cuffs and visible pockets, with balanced modest margins.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the jacket vertically and preserve its actual width, length, sleeve proportions and silhouette. Do not stretch it to match the benchmark.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Provide restrained three-dimensional shoulder and chest volume with a natural hollow neck opening. The support must remain entirely invisible.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve the uploaded lapels, gorge, closure, buttons, buttonholes, pockets, cuffs, vents, seams, lining edges and hem where evidenced.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve actual colour, pattern, weave, nap, sheen, wrinkles and tonal variation. Do not copy benchmark fabric or tailoring details.
- **Background:** Warm light neutral seamless studio background with no location scenery, person, hanger or unrelated props.
- **Lighting:** Soft even studio catalogue lighting with gentle directional shadows and readable material relief. No hard flash, colour gels or exaggerated sharpening.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not invent hidden lining, internal canvas, rear construction, vents, labels or hardware. Unseen details remain unknown.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no person, visible mannequin, support, text, watermark, collage or duplicate view.

## Review checks

Confirm that the support is invisible, the complete jacket is in frame, product proportions are credible and all visible tailoring matches the uploaded product references.
