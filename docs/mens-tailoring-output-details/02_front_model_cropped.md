## Benchmark observations

The benchmark is a composition reference for a cropped front model presentation. Its jacket colour, fabric, lapel construction, button count, shirt, trousers and model identity are not defaults for another product. The uploaded suit jacket remains authoritative.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded suit jacket worn by one adult model.
- Presentation: One adult model following the benchmark pose and crop; face and facial features excluded.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Level, straight-on front catalogue camera at torso height; avoid wide-angle distortion.
- **Framing and crop:** Base of neck through upper thighs, with the jacket collar, shoulders, sleeves, cuffs, front closure and hem visible where the benchmark permits. Exclude face, facial features and top of head.
- **Pose:** Natural upright stance with shoulders level, arms relaxed and hands away from the jacket's identity-critical details. No exaggerated twist or fashion pose.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the jacket and match benchmark occupancy without stretching the real garment proportions.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Preserve the uploaded shoulder structure, chest volume, waist suppression, hem shape, sleeve length and natural drape. Do not turn the jacket into a coat, tuxedo, waistcoat or full suit.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve the actual lapel or collar, gorge, closure, buttons, buttonholes, chest and lower pockets, cuffs, vents and seams where visible in the product references.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve the uploaded colour, pattern, weave, nap, sheen, wrinkles and tonal variation. Do not copy benchmark fabric or redraw texture.
- **Secondary styling:** Restrained neutral shirt, trousers and footwear may appear only as secondary styling. Do not let them compete with or determine the uploaded jacket.
- **Background:** Warm neutral seamless ecommerce studio background with no location scenery or unrelated props.
- **Lighting:** Soft even catalogue lighting with gentle directional shadows. No hard flash, colour gels, dramatic contrast or exaggerated sharpening.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not invent hidden lining, rear vents, labels, internal canvas, buttons or pocket construction. Unseen details remain unknown.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no collage, inset, duplicate view, text or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm that the face is excluded, the crop contains the intended jacket construction, styling remains secondary and no benchmark garment identity has been transferred.
