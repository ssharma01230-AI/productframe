# Seated Model

- **Global category:** `tailoring`
- **Eligible family:** `suit-jackets` only
- **Channel:** `ecommerce`
- **Presentation mode:** `model`; adult model with face and facial features excluded
- **Benchmark:** [View image](references/05.png)

## Benchmark observations

The benchmark supplies a restrained seated catalogue composition for showing jacket drape and fit. Its jacket identity, chair, model, styling and fabric are not defaults for another product.

## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded suit jacket worn by one adult model.
- Presentation: One adult model following the benchmark pose and crop; face and facial features excluded.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Ordinary level catalogue camera at torso height; never low-angle, voyeuristic or body-focused.
- **Framing and crop:** Match the benchmark's seated crop while keeping the jacket collar, shoulders, lapels, closure, sleeves, cuffs and relevant hem visible. Exclude face and facial features.
- **Pose:** Upright seated posture with knees together or naturally parallel, feet stable, shoulders level and hands resting neutrally away from the chest, hips and upper thighs. No arched back, spread legs or provocative gesture.
- **Furniture:** A simple chair may appear only as secondary support and must not compete with the jacket. No distracting props.
- **Product position and scale:** Preserve the jacket's real fit, length, sleeve proportions and drape over the seated posture. Do not pull, stretch or raise the hem.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Preserve the uploaded shoulder, chest, waist, hem and sleeve construction. Do not turn the garment into a coat, tuxedo, waistcoat or full suit.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve the actual lapels, gorge, closure, buttons, pockets, cuffs, vents and seams where evidenced.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve the uploaded colour, pattern, weave, nap, sheen, wrinkles and tonal variation. Do not copy benchmark texture or styling.
- **Background:** Warm neutral controlled studio setting with no location scenery.
- **Lighting:** Soft even catalogue lighting with modest contact shadows and readable fabric relief. No dramatic gels, hard flash or exaggerated sharpening.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not invent hidden lining, internal structure, chair contact effects, rear details or unsupported tailoring. Unseen details remain unknown.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph; no face, suggestive framing, collage, inset, duplicate view, text or watermark.

## Review checks

Confirm the pose is neutral and non-suggestive, the jacket remains the clear subject, the face is excluded and seated drape does not distort the product.
