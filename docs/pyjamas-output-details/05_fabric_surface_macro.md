## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** One continuous area of the uploaded pyjama product's actual fabric.
- **Presentation:** Material-only macro study with the textile arranged in soft rolling folds. No person, mannequin, hand, garment silhouette, swatch card or visible support.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Shallow oblique macro camera looking across the fabric surface so raised folds and valleys run diagonally through the portrait frame.
- **Framing and crop:** Fabric extends beyond all four image edges. Intentionally exclude collar, cuffs, waistband, pockets, fastenings, piping, seams, hems, labels and complete product outline unless a construction detail is explicitly part of the selected material sample.
- **Product position and scale:** Magnify the surface enough to resolve the real knit, weave, nap or finish while keeping the fibre scale credible. Broad folds occupy the full frame with no empty studio background.
- **Surface structure:** Preserve the uploaded textile's actual yarn direction, knit or weave scale, pile, grain, fuzz, smoothness, stretch behaviour and irregularities. Do not invent ribbing, fibres, brushing or a woven texture unsupported by the source.
- **Fold geometry and volume:** Use several soft diagonal ridges and shallow valleys consistent with the material's evidenced weight, thickness and flexibility. Do not make thin fabric heavy or structured fabric fluid.
- **Colour treatment:** Preserve the uploaded hue, saturation, tonal range, pattern, print and finish. Highlights and shadows may describe the folds but must not recolour the textile or transfer the benchmark navy.
- **Lighting:** Soft grazing studio illumination from one side, with gentle fall-off, restrained specular response and natural depth of field. Keep the focal surface readable without artificial oversharpening or synthetic microtexture.
- **Background:** None visible; the actual product fabric fills the entire image.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Show only surface properties supported by the uploaded references. Do not infer fibre composition, exact thread count, weight, softness, thermal performance or stretch percentage from appearance alone.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce macro photograph, approximately 4:5; no garment outline, model, hand, collage, inset, duplicate panel, text, measurement graphic or watermark.

## Review checks
