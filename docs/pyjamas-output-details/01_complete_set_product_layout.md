## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded pyjama product presented as a clean product-only ecommerce layout. Show both pieces only when the uploads establish that the product is a coordinated set.
- **Presentation:** Surface-supported garment arrangement with no person, mannequin, hanger, packaging or visible support. The garments retain shallow natural volume rather than appearing rigid or inflated.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Near-overhead front view, with the optical axis substantially perpendicular to the display surface and no wide-angle distortion.
- **Framing and crop:** Show the complete top and complete trousers with all identity-critical edges retained. Keep the collar or neckline, sleeves, cuffs, top hem, waistband, both legs and trouser hems inside the portrait frame.
- **Arrangement:** Place the top in the upper half, front facing upward, with sleeves extended naturally. Place the trousers behind or immediately below it, waistband near the top hem and legs extending downward. Preserve a readable separation between pieces without creating a collage or disconnected product grid.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the combined arrangement and use most of the frame while retaining modest warm-neutral margins. Preserve the real relative scale of the top and bottom.
- **Silhouette and volume:** Preserve the uploaded shoulder width, body length, sleeve shape, trouser rise, leg width, garment length and actual drape. Do not regularise the product into a generic button-front pyjama set.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve only evidenced neckline or collar, front opening, fastening count and spacing, pockets, waistband, drawstring or elastic construction, cuffs, piping or trim, seams and hems. Do not add benchmark piping, a chest pocket or buttons when absent.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve the uploaded colour, pattern, print placement, material appearance, knit or weave scale, sheen, wrinkles, wear and tonal variation. Do not transfer the benchmark navy fabric or white trim.
- **Background:** Warm light beige seamless studio surface, approximately `#C8C1B6`, with no props, furniture, bedding, scenery or horizon line.
- **Lighting:** Broad soft studio illumination with restrained contact shadows and readable material and edge detail. Avoid harsh highlights, crushed dark fabric and exaggerated sharpening.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not invent an absent matching piece, hidden rear construction, internal labels, waistband mechanism, pockets, fastenings or trim. Details not supported by the uploaded views remain unknown.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no person, visible support, collage, inset, duplicate view, text or watermark.
