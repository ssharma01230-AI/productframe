## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** Uploaded pyjama product worn by one adult model in a complete front-facing catalogue presentation.
- **Presentation:** Neutral full-length model view with face, facial features, eyes, nose, mouth, hairline and top of head excluded. No mannequin or second person.
- **Camera angle and orientation:** Level, direct straight-on camera at mid-body height with ordinary catalogue perspective and no tilt or wide-angle distortion.
- **Framing and crop:** Frame from the base of the neck or lower chin through both feet. Keep the complete top and trousers visible, including shoulders, sleeves, cuffs, top hem or overlap, waistband area, full legs and trouser hems.
- **Pose:** Upright symmetrical stance with shoulders level, arms naturally lowered, hands relaxed and feet modestly separated. Hands must remain outside the product silhouette where possible and must not cover pockets, openings or trim.
- **Product position and scale:** Centre the model and use most of the portrait height while retaining balanced margins. Preserve actual top-to-bottom proportions and garment length.
- **Silhouette and fit:** Preserve uploaded shoulder line, torso ease, sleeve shape, top length, waistband position, trouser rise, leg width and hem length. Do not slim, lengthen or regularise the pyjamas to match the benchmark model.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve evidenced neckline or collar, opening, fastening count and placement, pockets, piping or trim, cuffs, waistband, drawstring, seams and hems. Do not add benchmark construction absent from the upload.
- **Surface fidelity:** Preserve actual colour, pattern, print placement, weave or knit, finish, wrinkles, wear and tonal variation. Do not transfer benchmark navy fabric or white piping.
- **Secondary styling:** Use no footwear or accessories unless necessary for a clean catalogue presentation. If socks are used to match the benchmark stance, they must be plain, neutral and visually subordinate; do not copy them as product identity.
- **Background:** Warm light beige seamless studio background and floor, approximately `#C8C1B6`, with a restrained contact shadow and no props or scenery.
- **Lighting:** Soft even full-length catalogue lighting with readable dark materials and gentle dimensional shadows. No hard flash, colour gels or exaggerated sharpening.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** Do not invent a matching top or bottom, unseen rear construction, waistband mechanism, pockets, labels, fastenings or trim. Hidden details remain unknown.
- **Output:** One continuous portrait ecommerce photograph, approximately 4:5; no face, collage, inset, duplicate view, text or watermark.
