# Outerwear / Jackets — visually reviewed Output Details

All 9 Ecommerce benchmark images were opened and visually inspected. This reviewed pack is implemented for the `jackets` family only. Source mapping: `apps/web/app/studio/output-recipes.ts`, `OUTERWEAR_ECOMMERCE_RECIPES` and `getOutputRecipes`; backend mapping is in `services/api/src/productframe_api/generation_templates.py`. Coats and Gilets / Padded Vests remain intentionally unwired until reviewed benchmark packs exist.

Global category: `outerwear`; eligible family: `jackets` only. This Ecommerce set applies to jackets and does not apply to the Coats or Gilets / Padded Vests families. Benchmark observation: black biker-style jacket. Its leather, lapels, snaps, zips and panel pattern are observations only, not requirements for other uploaded jackets. Slot 03 is a rear shoulder close-up, not a full over-the-shoulder portrait; slot 05 is a fastening/edge macro, not a full product view.

## Interpretation rules

The benchmark controls composition, camera angle, framing and presentation—not product identity. Preserve the uploaded product's exact material appearance, texture scale, construction, colour, artwork and proportions. Only describe visible supported construction; do not invent hidden details or create synthetic microtexture to satisfy a macro crop. If the source cannot resolve the requested detail, a close-up source reference is needed for reliable fidelity. Background direction follows the existing review documents: light warm beige approximately #C8C1B6 wherever exposed; a material-only macro retains the product colour, not beige.

Numbered sections below are current frontend display slots. IDs and asset paths are retained exactly, even when filename numbers differ. This is an image-based review, not backend-generation verification.
