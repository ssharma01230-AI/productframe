## OUTPUT DETAILS

- **Subject:** One uploaded ties product, presented as the sole commercial focus.
- **Presentation:** Match this reference's looped product arrangement composition while keeping contextual clothing, body or support visually secondary. Do not add unrelated accessories, packaging or props.
- **Camera, framing and crop:** Near-overhead product view; include the complete loop, crossing blade, tail end and all outer edges.
- **Pose or arrangement:** Arrange one tie in a relaxed loop without tying a knot. Keep the blade facing outward and preserve natural fabric curvature.
- **Product position and scale:** Keep the product dominant and large enough to inspect, with clear separation from frame edges and no accidental cropping of identity-critical parts.
- **Silhouette, fit and drape:** Preserve the uploaded product's true proportions, structure, flexibility, thickness and gravity. Do not reshape it to imitate the benchmark.
- **Visible construction:** Preserve only evidenced tie type, blade and tail widths, length, tip shape, interlining and body, colour, pattern, weave, sheen, edge construction, keeper loop, label and branding. Do not invent benchmark-specific features or normalise the upload into a generic product.
- **Surface and colour fidelity:** Preserve exact uploaded colour, pattern placement and scale, material texture, weave or knit, grain, sheen, wear and tonal variation. Do not transfer the benchmark's navy woven necktie, white shirt and dark suit styling.
- **Background:** Clean seamless neutral studio setting matching the reference's restrained tonal character; no domestic or lifestyle scenery unless explicitly part of the composition.
- **Lighting:** Soft diffuse ecommerce lighting with controlled highlights, readable dark areas and gentle grounding or form shadows. Do not conceal construction or manufacture artificial texture.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** The reverse face, keeper and label need not be shown unless evidenced and naturally visible.
- **Output:** One continuous 1:1 square ecommerce image; no collage, inset, duplicate view, added text, source watermark or visible support unless the template explicitly requires contextual clothing.

## Review checks

Confirm the entire tie is in frame, the crossing is physically continuous and width transitions match the upload.
